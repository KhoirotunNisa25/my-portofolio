A web-based information system designed to facilitate centralized and transparent reporting and management of campus facility damages.

## Contributions

Developed the system’s features as a **Fullstack Developer** and designed the UI/UX to enhance the ease of reporting and data management for both staff and students.

## Key Features

* **Facility Damage Reporting Form:** A streamlined interface for submitting repair requests.
* **Admin Management Dashboard:** A centralized hub for tracking and processing reports.
* **Repair Status Notifications:** Real-time updates on the progress of reported issues.
* **Report History & Statistics:** Detailed logs and data insights for better infrastructure monitoring.
