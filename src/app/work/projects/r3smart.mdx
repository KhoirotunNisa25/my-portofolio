A marketplace platform for **buying and selling second-hand goods**, enhanced by AI to provide relevant product recommendations and promote a more sustainable recycling lifestyle.

## Design Focus

* **AI-Driven Product Discovery:** Personalized exploration based on smart recommendation algorithms.
* **Rapid Selling Flow:** A streamlined listing process featuring automated photo analysis and description generation.
* **Recycling & Impact Labels:** Clear visual indicators of the environmental impact and recycling potential for each product.
* **Integrated Communication:** A seamless, built-in chat system for direct interaction between buyers and sellers.
