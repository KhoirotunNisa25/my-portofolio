A web application that merges **AI and IoT** to provide personalized energy consumption recommendations, presented through a clean and easy-to-understand data dashboard.

## Design Focus

* **Real-Time IoT Dashboard:** Live energy consumption tracking pulled directly from connected IoT devices.
* **Informative Data Visualization:** Clear, readable charts and metrics designed for quick data interpretation.
* **AI-Powered Savings Recommendations:** Smart, actionable insights to help users optimize energy efficiency.
* **Abnormal Consumption Alerts:** Proactive notification system to detect and warn users of unusual energy spikes.
