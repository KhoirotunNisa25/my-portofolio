A desktop-based cashier application built to simplify sales operations and optimize product data management for enhanced business efficiency.

## Contributions

Developed the user interface using **Flutter**, with a core focus on usability and streamlined transaction flows. I ensured that every interaction is high-performance and intuitive, specifically tailored for the fast-paced environment of retail checkout.

## Key Features

* **Sales Transaction Management:** A seamless system for processing and recording sales.
* **Product & Inventory Tracking:** Centralized management for product data and real-time stock levels.
* **Daily Sales Reports:** Automated summaries to track business performance at a glance.
* **Intuitive & Responsive UI:** A desktop-optimized interface designed for speed and ease of use.
