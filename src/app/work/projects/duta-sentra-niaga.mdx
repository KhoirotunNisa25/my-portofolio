A professional company profile website designed for **Duta Sentra Niaga**, a company specializing in silica sand processing. The visual direction was crafted to reflect the company’s industrial strength and corporate identity.

## Contributions

Designed an **informative and professional** interface tailored to specific business requirements. I was responsible for the layout and visual hierarchy of the company profile, product catalog, and contact pages.

## Deliverables

* **Wireframes & High-Fidelity Designs:** Building the structural and visual foundation of the site.
* **Corporate Identity Design System:** Developing a cohesive set of components based on the company's branding.
* **Interactive Prototype:** Demonstrating user flow and navigational logic within Figma.
* **Visual Asset Guidelines:** Providing a manual for consistent use of imagery, icons, and typography.
