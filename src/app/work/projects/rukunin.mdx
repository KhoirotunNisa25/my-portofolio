A dedicated mobile platform built to streamline administration and enhance communication within local neighborhoods (RT/RW), integrating document management, financial tracking, and community events into one unified system.

## Contributions

Implemented the user interface with a **mobile-first** approach, ensuring accessibility and ease of use for a diverse demographic, including users who may have limited experience with digital technology.

## Key Features

* **Resident Administration & Documentation:** Digital management of essential neighborhood records.
* **Neighborhood Financial Management:** Transparent tracking of community funds and finances.
* **Announcements & Community Activities:** Real-time updates on local news and upcoming events.
* **Two-Way Communication:** An integrated channel to foster direct interaction between residents and local administrators.
