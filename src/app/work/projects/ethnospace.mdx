A design exploration for a mobile app dedicated to **traditional cultural equipment rentals**, prioritizing a user-friendly experience for finding and booking essential cultural items.

## Design Focus

* **Effortless Discovery:** Streamlined search for cultural equipment filtered by category and location.
* **Simplified Booking Flow:** A frictionless checkout process with minimal steps to completion.
* **Culturally Rich Visuals:** A warm visual palette designed to reflect local cultural identity and heritage.
* **Intuitive Onboarding:** A welcoming and easy-to-follow introduction for first-time users.
