A professional landing page for **NexaCodeStudio**, a digital development studio offering premium UI/UX design and web development services to potential clients.

## Contributions

Responsible for the end-to-end UI/UX process—from initial **wireframing to final implementation**. I developed the frontend with a core focus on creating a **professional, responsive** interface designed to effectively convert visitors into leads.

## Key Features

* **Impactful Hero Section:** Featuring a strong and clear Call to Action (CTA).
* **Service Portfolio Showcase:** A dedicated space to highlight past work and expertise.
* **Services & Core Strengths:** Structured sections detailing the studio's offerings and competitive advantages.
* **Responsive Design & Smooth Animations:** Fully optimized for all devices with fluid transitions for an enhanced user experience.
