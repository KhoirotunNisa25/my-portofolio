A digital platform for **reading and writing stories**, designed specifically to enhance the long-form reading experience and ensure effortless navigation between content.

## Design Focus

* **Distraction-Free Reading Mode:** A clean interface designed to minimize visual noise and focus on the narrative.
* **Optimized Typography:** Carefully selected font scales and spacing to maximize readability and reduce eye strain.
* **Seamless Chapter Navigation:** A fluid transition system for quick and intuitive browsing between story segments.
* **Author Profiles & Genre Discovery:** Dedicated spaces for creators and a structured exploration page for diverse literary categories.
