A health application design for **blood glucose monitoring**, specifically crafted to ensure users—including the elderly—can easily and independently track their health status.

## Design Focus

* **Clean & Readable Data Display:** A visual layout optimized for clarity, ensuring vital health metrics are easy to interpret.
* **Fast & Simple Measurement Input:** A streamlined data entry process designed for maximum efficiency.
* **Daily & Weekly Trend Graphs:** Comprehensive data visualization to help users track glucose patterns over time.
* **High/Low Glucose Alerts:** A robust notification system to provide immediate warnings for critical sugar levels.
