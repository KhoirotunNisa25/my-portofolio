A financial app design concept that leverages **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** to intelligently automate expense tracking and financial planning for users.

## Design Focus

* **Futuristic & Modern UI:** A visual style crafted to reflect advanced AI integration and innovation.
* **Conversational Onboarding:** A user-friendly onboarding flow utilizing a conversational interface for a more human touch.
* **Predictive Dashboard:** Advanced data visualization providing financial forecasts and personalized recommendations.
* **Behavior-Based Personalization:** Smart expense categorization that adapts to individual user spending habits.
