A financial app design that prioritizes **automation and deep analytics** as its core values, helping users understand their spending patterns with minimal effort.

## Design Focus

* **Smart Automated Categorization:** Effortless expense tracking powered by intelligent category mapping.
* **Visual Analytical Reports:** Detailed monthly and annual insights presented through intuitive data visualization.
* **Customizable Financial Widgets:** Personalized dashboard elements tailored to the user's priority metrics.
* **Budget Alert System:** A proactive notification flow designed to warn users when spending approaches or exceeds set limits.
